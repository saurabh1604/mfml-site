# Unit 11 · CINEMA PASS — rebuild every stage as a scene

## Why this document exists
The first build of Unit 11 shipped **diagrams of the arithmetic**: thin polylines on grids, bar charts, an empty square labelled "never lives here", a number line with two dots, walls of monospace readout. The owner's verdict: *"the visuals don't look awesome at all, the interactions are not great, nowhere near what we built so far."* The standard is Units 9–10: a lit landscape at scale, a character on it, props that ARE the analogy, a ▶ story with beats and a camera, HUD chips instead of paragraphs. **Every stage below is rebuilt to that standard.** The arithmetic is not touched — it is verified, and every readout string listed at the end must still print.

Read, in this order, before writing a line: this file · `tpl/u11-cinema.js` (the scene kit — you build with it, you never re-implement what it has) · `tpl/u11-shared.js` (the house runtime: `mountStage`, `hud`, `hint`, `tube`, `slot`, `overlay`, `liftedContours`, `grab`, `picker`, `tween`, `bindCtl`, `tabs`, `RM`, `easeIO`) · `tpl/cinema.js` (`CIN.stage3d`, `CIN.prim.*`, `CIN.ease`) · `tpl/u10-widgets.js` W7 `#w-salt`, W8 `#w-canyon`, W10 `#w-units` and its hero (the house style for a 3D story: how a stage is built, painted from state, tweened, and how the HUD reads) · the current `tpl/u11-widgets.js` (**the arithmetic and the readout strings you must keep** — take them, discard the drawing).

## The rules (hard — a stage that breaks one is sent back)
1. **The stage is the widget.** The stage column is `flex:3 1 460px`; the control column is `flex:1 1 230px`. The stage is `.stage3d.tall` (500 px) unless noted. On a phone it stacks; fine.
2. **Every stage is a scene, not a chart.** A scene has: (a) a lit landscape or floor from the kit (`valleyScene` or a floor of your own with lifted contours and a fading grid), (b) a **character** (`walkerChar`, `trolleyChar`, a box, a lantern…), (c) **props that are the analogy** (blocks, notebooks, a wall, a corridor, a string, rods…), (d) a **▶ story with named beats** and a **camera move** (`camTo`) in at least one beat, (e) a **flare** (`flare`) or **sparks** at the moment the point lands, (f) at most **four sprite labels** in-scene, (g) the live numbers in a **HUD chip** (`hudPair`), one line top, optional caption bottom.
3. **No bar charts. No side SVG charts. No empty panels.** A time series lives on a `glassPanel` at the back of the scene as a lit curve. A comparison of two things is two characters in one scene. If you catch yourself drawing rectangles with `el('rect',…)` to show a number, stop and put the number in the HUD.
4. **Readouts are ≤ 3 short lines** (`innerHTML` with `<b>` numbers, the exact strings listed at the end). Verdict is one line. `p.note` ≤ 2 sentences. The scene carries the meaning; the text confirms it.
5. **State lives outside `build()`.** `mountStage(box, build)` may call `build()` many times (theme flips; the stage is disposed when scrolled far away and rebuilt on return). `build()` reads the widget's state and repaints it; controls mutate state and call `paint()`; `paint()` returns at once if `sc` is null. Never keep a `THREE` object outside the `sc` closure.
6. **Colours** only from `ctx.colors` via `hxOf(ctx)` (`hx('s1')`…, `hx('critical')`) in three.js and `var(--sN)` in DOM. The five methods keep their colours everywhere: **GD s1 · Momentum s3 · AdaGrad critical · RMSProp s2 · Adam s4** (`M5`). The wall/fence/"what stops you" is **s2**; the feasible side / the fix is **s3**; the current point / the answer is **s4**; the second series is **s7**. Light theme: no additive glow, no point lights (the kit handles it) — test both.
7. **Motion** only through `tween` (respects reduced motion) or a `stage3d` `update` returning `false` under `CIN.reduced`. A ▶ story is 3–7 s, has ≥ 3 beats, restarts cleanly on a second press (keep a `run` token; ignore stale frames), and leaves the scene in a readable final state (not blank).
8. **Controls, ids, tabs and preset ids are unchanged** from the current `u11-widgets.html` (the verify script drives them). You may add a control; you may not rename or drop one. Tabs still switch the whole scene.
9. **Numbers first, then beauty.** Print the strings exactly as listed at the end. `fmt(v,0)` is broken — use `toFixed`. Minus is U+2212.
10. **Never write** MFML, ZC416, BITS or WILP anywhere.
11. **Performance:** `res ≤ 84` for surfaces; ≤ 400 lit tubes alive per scene; rebuild groups with `clearGroup`; call `RR(ctx)` after any change. A stage must build in < 300 ms.
12. **Choreography vocabulary:** *dolly* = `camTo(handle,{radius})`, *swing* = `{theta}`, *crane* = `{phi}` (phi small = from above, large = low). Start low and close for a race, crane up for a map, swing to show a wall face-on. Use `handle.setAutoRotate(0)` while a story runs and restore ≈ .08 after.

## World constants (shared by every Act-I stage)
`valleyScene(ctx,{c})` — the trough \(J=\tfrac12w_1^2+\tfrac c2 w_2^2\), \(w_1\in[-2.4,2.4]\), \(w_2\in[-1.3,1.3]\), c = 10 unless the stage has a stiffness dial. `V.at([w1,w2],lift)` gives the three.js position on the surface. Start [1,1]. All traces from `OPT.trace(kind,{c,alpha,N,beta,rho,rhoA,rhoF})` — **the only source of numbers**. Camera for a walk: `pos:[3.0,2.4,4.2], look:[0,.35,0], fov:36` (narrow screens: `[3.6,3.0,5.0]`). Props stand **outside the surface footprint** (|w1| > 2.5 or |w2| > 1.4) on the floor at `y = V.floorY`, or on a `glassPanel` at the back (`pos:[0,1.0,-1.85]`).

## HERO · `#hero-3d` — the race, then the wall (agent A writes it LAST, after W1–W5)
Loop ≈ 24 s, phases on `box.dataset.phase` (`race` → `wall` → `price` → `back`), under `CIN.reduced` show the `wall` frame statically.
- **race** (0–10 s). Camera starts **low and close behind the start** (`phi≈1.25, radius≈3.2, theta` looking down the trough) and dollies back and cranes up to `phi≈.95, radius≈5.6` over the phase. Five `walkerChar`s (M5 colours, r = .07) leave [1,1] together, stepping on the exact traces (≈ .38 s per step, 20 steps, `tween` per step), each with a `trailRibbon` and a small `cometTail` from its last position. GD dives then crawls; momentum overshoots to \(w_2=-0.9\) and rings; AdaGrad/Adam walk the diagonal; RMSProp sprints. When RMSProp's J < 1e-3, `flare` at the star and the HUD reads `RMSProp arrives · step 26`.
- **wall** (10–15 s). Trails fade to .25 (`ribbon.fade`), a `wallPanel` at \(w_1=1\) rises from the floor (`setH` 0 → 1 over .8 s) with a `flare` along its foot, `bannedFloor` darkens \(w_1<1\), and the star `tween`s along the surface from (0,0) to (1,0) and re-lights. HUD: `f* : 0 → 0.5 · the wall's price µ* = 1`.
- **price** (15–21 s). The wall slides \(1\to.6\to1\); the star tracks it along the surface; a `glassPanel` at the back draws \(f^\*=c^2/2\) as a lit curve with a bead that follows and a straight s3 tangent segment at c = 1; camera swings 40° to see the wall face-on.
- **back** (21–24 s). Wall `setH` → 0, floor lightens, star returns, loop.
`hud(box)` = `Five walkers · one valley` during race, `Then someone builds a wall` after. `hint(box,'drag to orbit')`.

## W2 · `#w-trolley` — the loaded trolley  (agent A)

## W11 · `#w-cases` — five conditions, a branching interrogation  (agent C)
**Scene (3D):** the floor is the map (lifted contours of \(x^2+2y^2\) on a flat floor, the allowed segment from (0,1) to (1,0) as a thick s3 tube). Above it rises the **case tree**: a lit trunk at the segment's midpoint splitting into 2ᵐ glowing branches (`tube`s, s1) that arc down to the four candidate points on the map (branch 4 to the origin, off the segment).
