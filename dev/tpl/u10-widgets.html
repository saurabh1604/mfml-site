Along a level curve \(F\) does not change, so for a tiny move \(\delta\mathbf x\) along it \(\nabla F\cdot\delta\mathbf x=0\): the gradient is perpendicular to the contour. On a round bowl "across the contour" and "toward the centre" coincide; on a stretched one they differ, and the difference is the zig-zag of the next widget's walker.
