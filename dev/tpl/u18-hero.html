Attention and Transformers

In Unit 17 a machine read one word at a time and squeezed the whole sentence into one small note. Here we throw that note away. Every word asks every other word a question, all at the same time. Each word gets a score for how well it answers — a plain dot product from Unit 3. Softmax from Unit 14 turns the scores into shares that add up to 1. Then each word takes a weighted blend of what the others offer. That is attention. It is Unit 13's kernel vote in new clothes. Stack it with a few simple parts and you have the transformer — the engine inside every modern language model.
