For each fixed \(x\), \(\mu\mapsto L(x,\mu)\) is a straight rod of height \(f(x)\) and slope \(g(x)\). The dual is the lowest of all those rods, and the lowest of any bundle of straight rods bends downward — so \(d\) is concave whatever \(f\) and \(g\) are. Weak duality \(d^*\le p^*\) is free; it is equality that needs convexity.
