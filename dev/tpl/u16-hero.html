Words as Vectors

A computer cannot read. To a computer, a word is just a label, like a code number in a shopkeeper's register. In this unit we give every word a short list of numbers — a place in space — so that words with similar meanings live close together. We start by counting which words follow which, and learn how to score a guesser. Then we count which words keep company, squeeze that big table with the SVD, and let a machine learn the lists by playing a guessing game. At the end, meaning turns out to be a direction: king − man + woman lands next to queen.
